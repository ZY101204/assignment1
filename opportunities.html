Here at Mount St. Joseph, we believe we have something for everyone, this includes in the theater progream. There are both classroom opportunities, as well as hands on opportunities for all.

Actor

If you've ever wanted to be in the bright lights, acting is the job for you! Actors learn roles and characters and bring them to life on stage. A season at MSJ typically sees a smaller play in the fall, and larger production in the Spring, which sometimes includes a Musical, so whether you like to sing or not, we have something for you.

Stage Management

The job of the stage manager is to run the show. They make sure that everything and everyone is set, and ready to go. They also call any cues for the show. Students can also fill the role of Assistant Stage Manager, and aid the SM in their duties.

Prop Crew

The job of the prop crew is to focus solely on the props that are used in the show. They ensure that all props are set at the beginning of the show and keep track of props that are used by actors throughout the show. The crew may also sanitize any props that require sanitization at the end of the show.

Costume Crew

The costume crew ensures that costumes are set and ready to go before every single show. They assist with any quick changes that may be set throughout the course of the show and are ready to fix any costume on the fly.

Stage Hand

The role of the stage hand is to move any furniture or props on and off the stage throughout the show.

Lighting Operator

The job of the lighting operator is to run any cues that the stage manager will call. Before and after every show the lighting operator will also test the lights for any burnt out bulbs.

Sound Operator

Similar to the lighting operator, the sound operators will run and cues called by the SM.

Set Building

The set building team assists the tech director in designing and building the set that will be shown on stage during the show. The set builders may also be asked to assist with loading the set in as well as painting any set pieces that may need painting.

Educational Opportunities

Here at Mount St. Joseph, we also offer classes that allow students to learn more about theatre in an educational aspect. These option include:

Theater Ensemble

Theater Ensemble is the main theater course. In this class students will read scripts, see shows, and have discussions about theatre. The class is a great way to explore your joy for theater and further your general knowledge

Acting Workshop

Acting workshops are offered every year at MSJ. Students will learn what it means to be an actor as well as how they can improve tyheir own acting abilities.

Directing Class

Once every two years a directing class is offered to students who may be interested in learning what exactly goes into directing a production. Students will even get the chance to direct their own one act play!

Sound and Lighting Class

Similar to the directing class, a lighting and sound class is offered once every two years for those who are interested in the techincal side of theatre. In this course students will learn all about what goes into light design and sound design, as well as the actual lights themselves.

Voice

Voice lessons are offered to everyone here at Mount St. Joseph, and we even have a Voice major, however, voice lessons are great for those who may be interested in Musical Theatre.
