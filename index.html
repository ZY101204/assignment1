The theatre program is a place where all different people come from many different backgrounds to celebrate their love for the arts. Here at Mount St. Joseph all undergraduate students and graduate students are welcomed and even encouraged to join us. Whether it be acting on stage, running the tech, or even managing the entire show, here at MSJ, we have something for everyone. We hope you join us during your journey here at Mount St. Joseph.
